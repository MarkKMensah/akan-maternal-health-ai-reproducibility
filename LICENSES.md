The repository combines original research materials with artefacts derived from separately licensed models and data.

- The upstream MMS and NLLB model repositories identify their licences as **CC BY-NC 4.0**. The derived MMS and RNMT adapters remain subject to those upstream terms.
- The source dataset article and deposit identify the dataset as **CC BY 4.0**. Consult the dataset DOI for the authoritative, current record and required attribution.
- No raw dataset files are redistributed here.
- Unless a file states otherwise, copyright in the original code and documentation is retained by its authors. The materials are available for academic inspection and reproducibility; no separate open-source licence is asserted for those files.

Commercial or clinical use requires independent review of the applicable upstream model, dataset, voice-data, ethical, and application obligations.
